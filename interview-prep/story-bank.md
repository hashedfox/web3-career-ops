# Story Bank — Master STAR+R Stories

This file accumulates your best interview stories over time. Each evaluation (Block F) adds new stories here. Instead of memorizing 100 answers, maintain 5-10 deep stories that you can bend to answer almost any behavioral question.

## How it works

1. Every time `/career-ops oferta` generates Block F (Interview Plan), new STAR+R stories get appended here
2. Before your next interview, review this file — your stories are already organized by theme
3. The "Big Three" questions can be answered with stories from this bank:
   - "Tell me about yourself" → combine 2-3 stories into a narrative
   - "Tell me about your most impactful project" → pick your highest-impact story
   - "Tell me about a conflict you resolved" → find a story with a Reflection

## Stories

### [Zero-to-One] Storacha GTM from Scratch
**Source:** Report #005 — Jito Foundation — Growth Lead: Special Project
**S (Situation):** Inherited a developer-focused product at Storacha (Protocol Labs) with no enterprise sales motion.
**T (Task):** Build the entire B2B go-to-market engine from zero.
**A (Action):** Defined 3 ICPs, deployed outbound infrastructure across Apollo, Salesforge, HubSpot, Clay, and n8n (6 automated sequences), built and led a team of 5, created weekly operating cadences and KPI dashboards.
**R (Result):** 15+ qualified enterprise prospects in 4 months from cold start. GTM strategy adopted by Filecoin Foundation for the FOC initiative.
**Reflection:** The zero-to-one muscle is the same whether the channel is outbound or social — the discipline is: define audience, build systems, measure, iterate.
**Best for questions about:** Building from scratch, initiative, systems thinking, leadership, resourcefulness

### [Community Building] Founded MUCC from Scratch
**Source:** Report #005 — Jito Foundation — Growth Lead: Special Project
**S (Situation):** No cryptocurrency education or community existed at the University of Melbourne.
**T (Task):** Create the first blockchain student organization from scratch.
**A (Action):** Recruited members, built external partnerships with industry professionals and analysts, organized expert panels, published original research on distributed consensus.
**R (Result):** Grew from founding idea to recognized university society over 2 years with regular event programming and external partnerships.

### [Ecosystem Scale] xStar 300+ Partner Integrations
